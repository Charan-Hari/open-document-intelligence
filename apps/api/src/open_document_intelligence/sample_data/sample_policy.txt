DATA RETENTION POLICY

Policy Owner: Information Governance Office
Review Cycle: Annual

1. Purpose. This policy defines how long operational records are retained
before secure disposal.

2. Scope. This policy applies to all business units handling customer or
employee records.

3. Retention Schedule. Financial records are retained for seven years.
Personnel records are retained for the duration of employment plus three
years. Support tickets are retained for two years.

4. Review. This policy is reviewed by the Policy Owner on the schedule
above, or sooner if regulatory requirements change.

5. Exceptions. Any exception to this policy must be approved in writing by
the Information Governance Office prior to implementation.
